# Project Brief: Stray Alert MVP

## 1. The problem
Pet owners struggle to quickly find lost pets, and veterinary clinics lack a centralized system to track registered pets and manage lost/found reports. Manual record-keeping makes it hard to reunite pets with their owners efficiently.

## 2. Users and what they need to do
| User role | What they need to do in the app |
|-----------|---------------------------------|
| Pet Owner | Register pets, generate QR tags, report lost pets, view pet profiles |
| Admin (Veterinary Clinic Staff) | Manage users, view all pets, resolve lost/found reports, manage system |
| Public | Report found pets, scan QR codes to identify pets, view lost/found feed |

## 3. The database behind it
Database: stray_alert (SQLite)
- **owners** - Stores pet owner information including admin role
- **pets** - Stores pet details, photos, and vaccination records
- **qr_tags** - Links pets to unique QR codes for identification
- **reports** - Stores lost and found pet reports with status tracking

## 4. Must-have features (for the final project)
- Register and manage pet profiles with photos
- Generate and print QR tags for pets
- Report lost or found pets with location and photos
- Scan QR codes to view pet information
- Admin dashboard to manage users, pets, and reports
- View lost/fold feed with search and filter

## 5. Nice-to-have features (only if there's time)
- Email notifications when a pet is reported found
- Map view of lost/found locations
- Statistics dashboard for clinic analytics

## 6. Similar apps I looked at
- **PawBoost** - Uses community network to find lost pets; I'll borrow their social sharing feature
- **Petfinder** - Clean pet profile layout with clear status badges; I'll borrow their card-based design
